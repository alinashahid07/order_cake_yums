# order_cake_yums

Well, what we have in here:
Its a simple game where user have to make cakes. 
It is inspired from that one game i used to play in childhood on my laptop. That was also cake making or something. 

In here, 
Customers come up to the counter with a cake order and you have to build it before they get bored/irritated and leave.

## How to play?!

Tho its pretty self obvious, but just for documentation:
So,
Read the ticket on the left, then use the tabs on the right (sponge, frosting, drip, toppings) to stack the cake from the bottom up. Hit Pack and Serve when you think it matches. If its right you get coins, plus a tip if the customer still has patience left. Get a few right in a row and your combo multiplier goes up. Tho, it gets reset, If a you served a wrong cake.

When you're done, press End Shift to get a receipt with your stats.

There's also a Sandbox mode with no timer and no orders, just for tryin out things as whatever cake you want.

## Stack Rules

We also have stack rules which mean what layer will go first and in what way.

- 6 layers max, and no more than 3 of them can be sponge
- up to 4 toppings, each one only once and they always on top
- sponges have to be in the same order as the ticket. while toppings can go in any order
